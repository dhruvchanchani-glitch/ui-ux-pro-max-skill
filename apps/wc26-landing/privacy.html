Privacy

Last updated: [date]. Have a lawyer review before launch.

What we collect

A username you choose, the team you support, your preferred timezone, your XP and Coin balances, a ledger of transactions (predictions placed, predictions resolved, perks purchased), auction history, and Draft Battle results. Plus anonymous identifiers for crash and performance diagnostics, your locale, and your screen size.

What we don't collect

Your contact list, photo library, microphone, or camera. No precise geolocation — we may resolve your timezone from a coarse IP lookup, but we don't store the IP. No real-money balances. XP top-ups and Supporter Pass subscriptions are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play; we never see your card details.

How we use it

To personalise the home screen, fixture list, and prediction markets. To resolve predictions and update the leaderboard. To prevent fraud in the coin economy (e.g. ensuring XP top-ups are legitimate via server-side receipt validation). To send you push notifications about your team's matches if you opt in.

Coin redemption

If you redeem Coins for a physical reward, we collect the minimum identity data required by prize-giving regulations and shipping. That data is deleted after the prize is fulfilled.

Your rights

You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Sign out → Delete data. Deletion is irreversible and removes your ledger, bets, and auction history within 30 days.

Children

The app is rated 12+ on Apple and Teen on Google because it involves in-app purchases. We do not knowingly collect data from users under that age.
