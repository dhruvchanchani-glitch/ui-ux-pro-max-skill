## Description body

WC26 is the companion app for the 2026 World Cup. Pick your team, and
every screen — the home, the match cards, the auction lobby — dresses
itself in your nation's colours.

**What you can do**

- See every fixture for your team in your local time.
- Place skill-based predictions on 7 markets per match: Match Winner,
  Top Scorer, MVP, Total Goals, First Scorer, Clean Sheet, Red Card.
- Build a streak. Five right in a row gets you a 50% Coin bonus.
- Stack 3+ predictions into accumulators when you feel sharp.
- Join a real-time multiplayer auction with €1B to spend on real
  players. Or play solo against AI.
- Take your auctioned squad into a Draft Battle and simulate a match.
- Spend Coins on cosmetics, betting boosts, auction perks — or save up
  and redeem for real merchandise.

**Two currencies, never mixed**

- XP buys you predictions. You get 500 XP free every day; top up if you
  want more.
- Coins are the reward currency. You earn them by being right. You
  cannot buy Coins — ever.

That's the deal. The leaderboard reflects genuine prediction skill, not
how much you've spent.
