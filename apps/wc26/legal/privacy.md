# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [date]. This is a starter template — have a lawyer review before launch._

## What we collect

- **Account data**: a username you choose, the team you support, your
  preferred timezone.
- **Gameplay data**: XP and Coin balances, ledger of transactions
  (predictions placed, predictions resolved, perks purchased), auction
  history, Draft Battle results.
- **Device data**: anonymous identifiers for crash and performance
  diagnostics, locale, screen size.

## What we don't collect

- Your contact list, photo library, microphone, or camera.
- Precise geolocation. We may resolve your timezone from a coarse IP
  lookup, but we don't store the IP.
- Real-money balances. XP top-ups and Supporter Pass subscriptions are
  processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play Store; we never see
  your card details.

## How we use it

- To personalise the home screen, fixture list, and prediction markets.
- To resolve predictions and update the leaderboard.
- To prevent fraud in the coin economy (e.g. ensuring XP top-ups are
  legitimate via server-side receipt validation).
- To send you push notifications about your team's matches if you
  opt in.

## Coin redemption

If you redeem Coins for a physical reward, we collect the minimum
identity data required by prize-giving regulations and shipping. That
data is deleted after the prize is fulfilled.

## Your rights

You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Sign out → "Delete
data". Deletion is irreversible and removes your ledger, bets, and
auction history within 30 days.

## Children

The app is rated for ages 12+ on Apple and "Teen" on Google because it
involves real-money in-app purchases. We do not knowingly collect data
from users under that age.
