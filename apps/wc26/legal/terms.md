_Last updated: [date]. This is a starter template — have a lawyer review before launch._

## What the app is

WC26 is a **skill-based prediction game** about the 2026 World Cup. You
spend a virtual currency called XP to place predictions on match
outcomes. Correct predictions earn a separate virtual currency called
Coins. Neither currency has cash value.

- XP can be purchased with real money via the App Store / Play Store.
- Coins **cannot** be purchased. They can only be earned by making
  correct predictions.
- Coins can be redeemed for digital rewards or, at specific high
  thresholds, physical merchandise. Redemption is at our discretion and
  subject to identity verification.

## What the app is not

- This is **not** a real-money gambling product. We do not pay cash
  prizes. Your XP balance is not redeemable for cash. Your Coin balance
  is not redeemable for cash.
- We are not affiliated with FIFA or any team featured in the app.

## Your account

You're responsible for keeping your account secure. We reserve the
right to suspend accounts that engage in fraud (e.g. exploiting bugs to
mint XP, attempting to manipulate odds or simulation results, or
purchasing accounts).

## Eligibility

You must be at least 13 (or your country's digital age of consent,
whichever is higher) to use the app, and 18+ to redeem Coins for
physical rewards.

## Service changes

We may change odds bands, payout multipliers, and perk pricing at any
time. We won't unilaterally remove XP or Coin balances except in cases
of fraud.

## Limitation of liability

The app is provided "as is". We're not liable for incidental losses
arising from use of the app — for example, a missed kickoff because the
local-time conversion was wrong.
